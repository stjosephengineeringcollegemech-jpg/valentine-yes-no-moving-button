=====================================================
HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS FOR PRANAMYA'S BIRTHDAY SLIDES
=====================================================

1. Location to put your photos:
   Folder: d:\abhith\valentine-yes-no-moving-button\slides\

2. File naming format:
   Name each image starting with the number (1 to 6), followed by your tagline!
   
   Examples:
   - 1The Day We Met.jpg          -> Tagline shown: "The Day We Met"
   - 2Our First Coffee Date.png   -> Tagline shown: "Our First Coffee Date"
   - 3Always Laughing Together.jpg-> Tagline shown: "Always Laughing Together"
   - 4Late Night Adventures.png   -> Tagline shown: "Late Night Adventures"
   - 5Forever My Favorite.jpg     -> Tagline shown: "Forever My Favorite"
   - 6To A Lifetime of Joy.jpg    -> Tagline shown: "To A Lifetime of Joy"

3. Supported image formats:
   .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif

4. Automatic update:
   Whenever you add or change photos in this folder, you can run:
      python update_manifest.py
   
   The app will automatically read your photos and display your tagline 
   as burning, glowing handwritten calligraphy on the old parchment note!
